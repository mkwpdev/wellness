A little of my story

Why a
four-leaf clover?

As a sensitive kid, I found four-leaf clovers on the playground and gave them to my classmates. Those little discoveries became a way to connect.

Years later, I found a whole patch across the street from my New Orleans home. Inspired by Dr. Andrew Weil, I made a collage spelling Believe and gave it to him in person.

Once you believe, you begin to see.
